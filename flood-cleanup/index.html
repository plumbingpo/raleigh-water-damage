Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Raleigh Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Raleigh to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Raleigh

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
