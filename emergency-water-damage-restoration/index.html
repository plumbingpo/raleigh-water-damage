When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Raleigh

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
